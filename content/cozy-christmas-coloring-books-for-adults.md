# Cozy Christmas Coloring Books for Adults: Bold & Easy Picks for 2026

*By Wesley Lopes, economista*

> **Note:** One book on this list, *Cozy Christmas Around the World*, is published by Cozy World Press, the imprint behind this site.

## The short answer

If you want a relaxing Christmas coloring book you can actually finish, pick a **"bold and easy" book with single-sided pages**. Bold, thick outlines make each page doable in one evening, and single-sided printing means marker bleed lands on a blank back instead of ruining the next picture. Good options this season:

- **Easiest, cutest scenes:** *Cozy Cheer* by Cherry Lam (40+ bold, single-sided scenes, $9.99 list).
- **Premium gift:** *Christmas Colors* by Maria Trolle (hardcover, 40 removable single-sided illustrations).
- **Detail lovers:** *Johanna's Christmas* by Johanna Basford (37 single-sided, perforated designs).
- **Holiday travel theme:** *Cozy Christmas Around the World* by Lily Atwood (50 bold & easy scenes from around the globe, single-sided, 8.5 x 8.5 in).

The rest of this guide explains why "cozy" coloring took off, what to check before you buy, and how to pick the right book for you or as a gift.

## Why cozy coloring books are everywhere right now

Adult coloring is having its second big moment. The Conversation reported in February 2026 that two of Australia's top ten bestselling books in 2025 were adult coloring books, *Cozy Corner* and *Cozy Cuties* by Coco Wyo, and that "cozy coloring book" is a trend on BookTok ([The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/adult-colouring-books-are-bestsellers-again-it-makes-sense-in-our-anxious-age-275216)).

The style has changed since the 2015 boom. Back then, bestsellers like *Secret Garden* were intricate patterns. Today's cozy bestsellers are "much simpler, even childlike," with broad outlines and large shapes that take minimal effort, pitched at adults who want comfort and rest rather than a challenge (same source). The article links this to a shift toward low-pressure, offline activities in response to screen saturation.

Coloring book artist Sarah Renae Clark describes the same resurgence of "bold and easy" and "cosy colouring" books, along with online techniques and challenges, especially with alcohol markers ([Sarah Renae Clark](https://sarahrenaeclark.com/cosy-colouring-15-trends-taking-over-adult-colouring-in-2026/)).

For Christmas, this matters: a cozy holiday book is one people actually use in December, often while a movie plays, instead of one that sits half-finished.

## What to check before you buy

### 1. Single-sided pages

This is the most important feature if you use markers. One reviewer at Colored Calm argues that "the artwork matters far less than the paper," and that paper weight and single-sided printing decide whether markers are even an option ([Colored Calm](https://coloredcalm.com/best-christmas-coloring-books-2026-6-cozy-titles-under-25-dollars-that-are-in-stock-now/)). Look for "single-sided" in the product description. If it isn't listed, assume it isn't.

### 2. Line weight: bold and easy vs. detailed

- **Bold and easy:** thick outlines, big shapes, finish a page in one sitting. Best for relaxing, beginners, tired eyes, or chunky markers.
- **Detailed:** fine lines and small spaces. Better for colored pencils and people who enjoy long sessions.

Neither is better. Pick the one that matches how you color.

### 3. Paper and bleed-through

Even single-sided books vary. Colored Calm notes that a popular $7 cozy Christmas book has thin pages that alcohol markers bleed through, and suggests tucking two sheets of printer paper behind the page as a blotter ([Colored Calm](https://coloredcalm.com/best-christmas-coloring-books-2026-6-cozy-titles-under-25-dollars-that-are-in-stock-now/)). That's a cheap habit worth keeping with any book.

### 4. Page size and count

Square 8.5 x 8.5 in books are common in the cozy niche; letter-size (8.5 x 11 in) gives more room. Count matters less than whether you'll enjoy the scenes. 30 pages you love beats 80 you skip.

### 5. Timing

If it's a gift, order early. Colored Calm points out that this season's hardcovers landed between August and October, and that after Thanksgiving popular titles sell through and delivery estimates can slide past December 25 ([Colored Calm](https://coloredcalm.com/best-christmas-coloring-books-2026-6-cozy-titles-under-25-dollars-that-are-in-stock-now/)).

## Tips to make holiday coloring more relaxing

- **Keep a blotter sheet** behind every page, even in single-sided books.
- **Test your markers** on the inside back cover or a scrap first.
- **Pick a small palette** (3 to 5 colors) to avoid decision fatigue. Pastels suit cozy scenes.
- **Add white highlights** at the end with a white gel pen or paint marker. Sarah Renae Clark uses this to tie together even messy "chaos coloring" pages ([source](https://sarahrenaeclark.com/cosy-colouring-15-trends-taking-over-adult-colouring-in-2026/)).
- **Frame a favorite.** Single-sided and perforated pages make finished work easy to display or give as a handmade card.

## FAQ

### What is a "bold and easy" coloring book?
It's a coloring book with thick outlines and large, simple shapes, designed so adults can finish a page in one sitting with little effort. The Conversation describes today's cozy bestsellers as rendered "in broad outlines and large shapes that can be coloured with minimal effort" ([source](https://theconversation.com/adult-colouring-books-are-bestsellers-again-it-makes-sense-in-our-anxious-age-275216)).

### Are Christmas coloring books good for adults or only for kids?
Many are made for adults. The cozy style looks simple, but titles like Coco Wyo's are "clearly pitched at adults," with the focus on comfort and rest (same source).

### Why does single-sided matter?
Markers, especially alcohol markers, can bleed through paper. With single-sided pages, the bleed lands on a blank back instead of the next design.

### What markers or pencils should I use?
Colored pencils work in any book. For markers, choose a single-sided book with thick paper, and put a blotter sheet behind each page.

### Is a coloring book a good stocking stuffer?
Square paperbacks around 8.5 x 8.5 in fit easily in a stocking. Pair one with a small set of pencils or markers.

### When should I order a Christmas coloring book?
Before Thanksgiving if you can. Popular titles can sell through and delivery estimates can slip past December 25 ([Colored Calm](https://coloredcalm.com/best-christmas-coloring-books-2026-6-cozy-titles-under-25-dollars-that-are-in-stock-now/)).
