About Cozy World Press

Cozy World Press is a small independent imprint of bold & easy coloring books for adults and teens. Our books are illustrated collections published under the pen name Lily Atwood.

Our guides are written by Lily Atwood. Every fact links to its source. When we mention one of our own books, we say so.
